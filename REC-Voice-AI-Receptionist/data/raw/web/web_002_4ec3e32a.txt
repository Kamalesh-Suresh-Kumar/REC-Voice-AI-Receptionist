Introduction
Established in
2005
, the Department of Aeronautical Engineering offers a
4-year B.E. programme
with an intake of 60 students, shaping future leaders in aviation, aerospace, and emerging technologies.
Our programme blends core aeronautical disciplines—structural mechanics, aerodynamics, propulsion, and space dynamics - with cutting-edge applications in
aircraft engineering, drones, rockets, and space technology
. Students also gain strong foundations in mathematics, physics, chemistry, and humanities, ensuring a well-rounded engineering education.
Hands-on learning
is at the heart of our approach. The department houses
state-of-the-art laboratories
including:
Aircraft Propulsion Lab
Aerodynamics Lab
Aircraft Structures Lab
Aircraft Simulation Lab
Design, Modelling & Analysis Lab
High-Speed Jet Lab
These facilities support both academic learning and advanced research. Computational skills are developed through
programming, simulation and industry-standard engineering software
.
We maintain strong industry and research collaborations with organizations like
NAL and ISRO
, enabling students and faculty to engage with real-world aerospace challenges. Final-year students undertake year-long, industry-relevant projects, often supported by leading MNCs and defence laboratories, meeting the
high standards of modern aerospace engineering
.
Career Pathways
Graduates find opportunities in
civil aviation, defence R&D, space research, CSIR laboratories
and software industries specializing in engineering tools. Many also excel in management, civil services, higher education, and research at M.Tech and Ph.D. levels.
Postgraduate Programme - M.E. Avionics
Launched in
2018
, the M.E. Avionics programme equips students with expertise in integrating avionics subsystems, cockpit systems, and next-gen aerospace technologies. With a strong focus on
drone technology, AI integration, and advanced flight systems
, the programme aligns with global aviation standards and industry demands.
Career Opportunities
M.E. Avionics graduates are in demand with
DRDO, ISRO, ADE, CVRDE
and leading companies like
Honeywell, GE, and Data Patterns
. They are also sought after in engineering software development and the rapidly expanding drone industry, including roles as
certified Drone RC pilots
for civil and military applications. The programme also fosters an entrepreneurial mindset, encouraging ventures in unmanned aerial systems and aerospace innovation.
