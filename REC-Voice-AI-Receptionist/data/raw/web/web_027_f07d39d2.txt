PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES
PEO I To provide students with sound foundation in the mathematical, scientific and engineering fundamentals necessary to formulate, analyze and solve engineering problems and to prepare them for postgraduate studies and for successful careers in industries.
PEO II To develop the ability among students to define engineering problems in the fields of Electronics and Communication Engineering, and to employ necessary techniques, hardware, and communication tools for modern Engineering applications.
PEO III To instill the values, skills, leadership and team spirit for comprehensive and wholesome personality, to promote entrepreneurial interest among students and to create a fervor for use of Engineering in addressing societal concerns.
PROGRAMME OUTCOMES
PSO1 An ability to formulate solutions for practical societal requirements using communication engineering.
PSO2 To design and formulate solutions for industrial requirements using Electronics and Communication Engineering.
PSO3 To understand and develop solutions required in multidisciplinary engineering fields.

Graduates of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) have vast opportunities in cutting-edge and rapidly evolving fields such as semiconductors, wireless communication systems including 5G and 6G networks, microsensors, and the Internet of Things (IoT). This dynamic and versatile branch of engineering plays a crucial role in a wide array of sectors, including automotive systems, consumer electronics like smartphones, high-speed computer processors, and biomedical instruments.
The ECE department is supported by a team of highly qualified, experienced, and dedicated faculty members who bring strong academic and practical knowledge to the classroom. In addition, well-trained and committed technical staff efficiently manage and assist in the operation of the various laboratories.
The curriculum encompasses a broad spectrum of subjects, including analog and digital electronics, Very Large Scale Integration (VLSI), Micro Electro Mechanical Systems (MEMS), and Radio Frequency (RF) and wireless communication technologies. Graduates are well-equipped to pursue rewarding careers in prominent electronics and communication companies both nationally and internationally.
Moreover, the field offers extensive opportunities for research and lifelong learning. To enhance students’ practical exposure and skills, the department emphasizes industry interaction through initiatives like industrial visits, participation in technical hackathons, and encouragement for publishing research in journals and conferences.
