Introduction
Programmes Offered
UG - B.E. Civil Engineering
The Civil Engineering Department was started in the year 2009 with an intake of 60 students. The intake has been increased to 120 students in the year 2014. The current intake is 60 students from the year 2021. The Department is continuously accredited by NBA from 2019 to till date. The department has an eminent faculty team with specialization in Structural Engineering, Soil Mechanics, Environmental Engineering, Irrigation and Water resources, Coastal Management, Construction Engineering & Management and Transportation Engineering. Our students have excelled in the Anna University exams and have secured Anna University ranks. The department has been sanctioned with sponsored research projects by DST-SERB, DST-WMT, DST-TDP, MSME & DRDO-NRB and established Centre of Excellence in “Sustainable Construction Materials"..
Vision
To be a department imparting knowledge in Civil Engineering education, research, entrepreneurship and industry outreach services for creating sustainable infrastructure and enhancing quality of life with professional and ethical values.
Mission
To provide an effective teaching – learning environment enabling students to be a competent Civil Engineer.
To motivate research and entrepreneurial initiatives in the field of Civil Engineering.
To inculcate ethical values to serve the society with high order professionalism.
