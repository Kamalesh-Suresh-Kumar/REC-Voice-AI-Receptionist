ERP AND ADMINISTRATION

Student support services are managed through the ERP software named REC Unified Portal.

The REC Unified Portal provides access to subject registration, subject-wise attendance, internal test marks and final grades.

Bonafide Certificates, Fee Certificates, Transfer Certificates and LoR can be generated through the portal developed by the College.

The administrative structure of the college is operated through ERP software named REC Unified Portal.

REC Unified Portal modules include:
Student Attendance
Assessment
Staff Attendance
Library
Transport
Schedule of Events
Examinations
Hostel
Stock and Inventory


LIBRARY POLICY

The Central Library at Rajalakshmi Engineering College holds more than 61,000 books on science, technology, engineering, management, politics, philosophy and general studies.

Students and faculty members are given remote access to the e-resources available in the Library.


PLACEMENT POLICY

Training and Placement Cell.

The Training and Placement Cell organizes training programmes and residential training camps.

Offer Category:

Core offer - Core/Manufacturing/production/civil/and any department specific offer.

Super Dream offer - 6.5 LPA above.

Dream offer - 4.5 LPA.

General - Less the 4.5 LPA.

A student placed with a General offer can apply for the next placement for a company which offers Dream, Super Dream and Core offer.
