SECTION: Introduction
QURA Film Academy is a premier filmmaking institute in Chennai dedicated to shaping the next generation of storytellers, creators, and cinema professionals. Established through a unique collaboration between Qube Cinema , Rajalakshmi Institutions , and LevelUp Learning , the academy delivers an immersive, industry-oriented education that bridges creative vision with real-world filmmaking practices. Students learn under the guidance of accomplished filmmakers and industry experts while gaining hands-on experience through live productions and collaborative projects.
The academy offers an 11-month Certificate in Filmmaking , designed to help students master every stage of the filmmaking process—from concept development and storytelling to production and post-production. The curriculum includes practical training in direction, cinematography, screenplay writing, editing, sound design, production management, visual storytelling, and film appreciation , ensuring graduates develop both creative and technical expertise. Throughout the program, students work on multiple film projects, build a professional portfolio, and gain valuable industry exposure.
With state-of-the-art infrastructure, a curriculum driven by industry needs, and a strong emphasis on experiential learning, QURA Film Academy prepares aspiring filmmakers to confidently pursue careers in cinema, OTT platforms, advertising, digital content creation, and other creative industries. Whether your passion lies behind the camera or in bringing stories to life on screen, QURA provides the knowledge, mentorship, and practical experience to transform your passion into a profession
