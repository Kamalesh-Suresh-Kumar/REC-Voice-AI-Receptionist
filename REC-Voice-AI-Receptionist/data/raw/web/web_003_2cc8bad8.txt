About
The Office of the Controller of Examinations is equipped with the the state-of-the-art technology infrastructure for effective functioning. The Office of the CoE is located in the Workshop Block of the College. The office is spacious and air-conditioned, and has a large amount of storage capacity to store question papers, answer sheets and examination materials. The office has an attached store room.
