SECTION: Introduction
Established in 2005 , the Department of Aeronautical Engineering offers a 4-year B.E. programme with an intake of 60 students, shaping future leaders in aviation, aerospace, and emerging technologies.
Our programme blends core aeronautical disciplines—structural mechanics, aerodynamics, propulsion, and space dynamics - with cutting-edge applications in aircraft engineering, drones, rockets, and space technology . Students also gain strong foundations in mathematics, physics, chemistry, and humanities, ensuring a well-rounded engineering education.
Hands-on learning is at the heart of our approach. The department houses state-of-the-art laboratories including:
Aircraft Propulsion Lab
Aerodynamics Lab
Aircraft Structures Lab
Aircraft Simulation Lab
Design, Modelling & Analysis Lab
High-Speed Jet Lab
These facilities support both academic learning and advanced research. Computational skills are developed through programming, simulation and industry-standard engineering software .
We maintain strong industry and research collaborations with organizations like NAL and ISRO , enabling students and faculty to engage with real-world aerospace challenges. Final-year students undertake year-long, industry-relevant projects, often supported by leading MNCs and defence laboratories, meeting the high standards of modern aerospace engineering .
Career Pathways
Graduates find opportunities in civil aviation, defence R&D, space research, CSIR laboratories and software industries specializing in engineering tools. Many also excel in management, civil services, higher education, and research at M.Tech and Ph.D. levels.
SECTION: Postgraduate Programme - M.E. Avionics
Launched in 2018 , the M.E. Avionics programme equips students with expertise in integrating avionics subsystems, cockpit systems, and next-gen aerospace technologies. With a strong focus on drone technology, AI integration, and advanced flight systems , the programme aligns with global aviation standards and industry demands.
Career Opportunities
M.E. Avionics graduates are in demand with DRDO, ISRO, ADE, CVRDE and leading companies like Honeywell, GE, and Data Patterns . They are also sought after in engineering software development and the rapidly expanding drone industry, including roles as certified Drone RC pilots for civil and military applications. The programme also fosters an entrepreneurial mindset, encouraging ventures in unmanned aerial systems and aerospace innovation.

PROGRAMME EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES
Our graduates have the ability to apply knowledge across the disciplines and in emerging areas of Aerospace Engineering for higher studies, research, employability and product development.
Our graduates have the communication skills, sense of responsibility to protect the environment and ethical conduct towards their profession and commitment to serve the society.
Our graduates possess academic excellence, managerial skills, leadership qualities and understand the need for lifelong learning for a successful professional career.
PROGRAMME OUTCOMES (POs)
Engineering Graduates will be able to,
Engineering knowledge
: Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.
Problem analysis
: Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.
Design/development of solutions
: Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet the specified needs with appropriate consideration for the public health and safety, and the cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.
Conduct investigations of complex problems
: Use research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of the information to provide valid conclusions.
Modern tool usage
: Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools including prediction and modeling to complex engineering activities with an understanding of the limitations.
The engineer and society
: Apply reasoning informed by the contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to the professional engineering practice.
Environment and sustainability
: Understand the impact of the professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, and demonstrate the knowledge of, and need for sustainable development.
Ethics
: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of the engineering practice.
Individual and team work
: Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams, and in multidisciplinary settings.
Communication
: Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and with society at large, such as, being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations, and give and receive clear instructions.
Project management and finance
: Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the engineering and management principles and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.
Life-long learning
: Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

(HAL), which has its factories at Bangalore, Nashik, Koraput, Kanpur etc., Defence Research and Development Organization (DRDO), National Aerospace Laboratories (NAL), Aeronautical Development Establishment, Aeronautical Development Agency, Civil Aviation Department etc. The Defence services such as Indian Air Force (IAF), Indian Navy (IN), and Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) also happen to be three major employers.
This course trains an engineer in designing, constructing, analyzing, testing, developing and manufacturing of commercial and military aircrafts, missiles and spacecrafts. Aeronautics focuses on systems that operate on the earth's atmosphere and astronautics on those operating in space. Aerospace engineers design, develop, test, and help manufacture commercial and military aircraft, missiles, and spacecraft. They develop new technologies in commercial aviation, defense systems, and space exploration.
Centre of Excellence for High Speed Jet Lab
The High Speed Jet Facility started in the year 2012 -13 with a seed money of Rs.12.5 L.The High Speed Laboratory provides facilities in the areas of High Speed Jet Mixing and Acoustics, Supersonic. Combustion and so on. In addition to catering to the needs of undergraduate laboratory course needs, it caters to the research requirements of a large number of Doctoral (PhD) and Master (MTech) students.The lab is equipped with an indigenously built Jet Acoustic Chamber.
